Watchers receive in-app notifications (and optional digest emails) when a ticket receives new comments, column moves, assignee changes, priority changes, or description edits.

## Watch, Unwatch, and Mute

On any ticket you have permission to view:

- Click **Watch** in the ticket panel header to start watching, or **Watching** to stop.
- Click the **watcher count** next to the toggle to inspect the current watchers list (names and avatars only; paginated beyond 20).
- Click the **bell icon** next to **Watching** to **mute** watched-update notifications for that ticket while remaining on the watcher list.
- Tickets you are watching display an eye indicator on Kanban cards.

## Auto-Watch Rules

In **Account Settings → Notifications**, you can toggle auto-watching independently for:

- Creating a ticket
- Being assigned to a ticket
- Commenting on a ticket
- Being `@mentioned` on a ticket

If you explicitly unwatch a ticket, later comments or edits will not re-subscribe you automatically unless you are newly assigned or `@mentioned`.

## Slash Commands and Search

- `/watch [ticket]` — Watch a ticket (defaults to the currently open ticket).
- `/unwatch [ticket]` — Stop watching a ticket (defaults to the currently open ticket).
- `/mute [ticket]` — Toggle muted notifications on a ticket (defaults to the currently open ticket).
- `watching:me` — Filter search results in the Spotlight bar (`Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K`) to tickets you watch.
