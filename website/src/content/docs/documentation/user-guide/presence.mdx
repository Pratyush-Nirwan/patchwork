---
title: Real-Time Presence & Typing
description: See who is currently viewing or typing on a ticket, and control your presence privacy.
---

When you open a ticket, Patchwork shows lightweight real-time presence:

- **Viewer avatars:** Up to 5 avatars of other team members currently viewing the ticket appear in the ticket header (with a `+N` overflow badge and name tooltip). Multiple tabs of the same user are counted once.
- **Typing indicator:** When someone types in the comment editor, `"Alex is typing…"` appears above the comment box. Draft text is never transmitted or stored.

## Ephemeral Lifecycle

- View heartbeats run every 15 seconds while the tab is visible and pause automatically when the tab is hidden.
- Typing heartbeats are throttled to once every 3 seconds and expire after 5 seconds of inactivity.
- Closing a tab sends an immediate leave beacon; stale viewers expire after 35 seconds.
- Presence state is kept strictly in memory and never written to the database.

## Privacy Controls

- **Personal toggle:** Disable **"Show when I'm viewing or typing on a ticket"** in **Account Settings → Notifications**.
- **Organization toggle:** Administrators can disable presence globally in **System Configuration → Notifications → General & Privacy**.
