<Aside type="note">
**Role note**: Viewers can browse boards and switch between Board and Calendar views. Members can move tickets between columns. Only Admins and Team Leads can create, rename, delete, or drag-reorder Kanban columns.
</Aside>

## Signing In & Password Changes

1. Open Patchwork and sign in with your email and password.
2. If your account was imported with a temporary password, Patchwork prompts you to set a new password before opening the workspace.
3. To change your password later, open **Account Settings** from the bottom-left user menu in the navigation rail.

## Navigating the Workspace

Use the left navigation rail to switch between:

- **Teams** you belong to
- **Projects** and their **Boards**
- **Board** vs. **Calendar** view toggle in the top bar
- **System Configuration** (Admins only)

Toggle the navigation rail with `[` (customizable in Keyboard Shortcuts).

## Kanban Columns & WIP Limits

- **Creating & editing columns** (`admin` / `lead`): Click **Add Column** in the top bar or edit a column header to set a name, color, and optional WIP limit.
- **Reordering columns** (`admin` / `lead`): Drag the column grip handle horizontally to reorder columns.
- **WIP capacity gauge**: Columns with a WIP limit show a progress track under the column title that turns red when the ticket count exceeds the limit.

## Expected Result

Board changes persist immediately in PostgreSQL and synchronize in real time to other team members via SSE.
